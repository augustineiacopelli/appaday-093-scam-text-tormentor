Before you send anything

Replying tells them the number is live. A working number is worth money to a scammer and gets sold on. Expect more junk if you engage, and use a burner or secondary number if you have one.

Never send real information. No name, address, bank details, codes, photos, or payments, no matter how funny the setup gets. Do not tap their links.

Report it too. In the US you can forward the message to 7726, which spells SPAM, and file a report at reportfraud.ftc.gov. If someone you know is actually losing money, stop joking and help them contact their bank.
